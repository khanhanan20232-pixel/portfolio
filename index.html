<h1>

About me

</h1>
<p>

I’m a web developer based in Garhi Afghanan, Wah Cantt, Pakistan, and part of Team Abdul Hanan Khan at FG Science Degree College for Men, Wah Cantt. I taught myself modern web development by building real projects — not just tutorials — and my main focus is education: tools that genuinely help students study smarter.

My flagship project is FBISE Study Hub, a complete exam-preparation platform for Federal Board (FBISE) students with 3,400+ MCQs, past and model papers, smart flashcards, study groups and an AI paper checker — live at fbise-study-hub.vercel.app.

Alongside web development, I also run Islamic content channels and experiment with AI-generated video — scripting, voicing and editing faceless series built entirely with AI tools.

</p>

<h1>

My Skills

</h1>
<p>

I build complete websites — from the first sketch of an interface to a live, database-backed web app. My everyday stack is HTML, CSS, JavaScript and TypeScript on the front end, with React and Next.js for interfaces and Node.js, Prisma and PostgreSQL behind them.

FBISE Study Hub runs on exactly this stack: 360+ quizzes, flashcards with spaced repetition, study groups and an AI paper checker, all designed in a lime, cream & black theme I created and applied across every page.

I care about pages that load fast on slow connections, layouts that hold up on a phone screen, and details — sidebars, login screens, themes — that make a site feel finished.
